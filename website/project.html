Gujral Hills, Ranjhi

A meticulously planned township in Mohania, Ranjhi — offering a peaceful lifestyle with all modern conveniences. Unlike the rest of Ranjhi, Gujral Hills is built to give you a better quality of life without leaving your roots behind.

We offer plots ranging from 400 sq. ft. to over 1,000 sq. ft. along with commercial shops, all within a secure gated community with paved roads, drainage, and reliable utilities.
