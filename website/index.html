Why Choose Gujral Hills?

TNCP & RERA-Approved

Offering a variety of plot sizes ranging from 400 sq. ft. to over 1,000 sq. ft. and commercial shops to suit your needs.

Live Sustainably in a Secure Community

Gujral Hills is a gated colony with closed boundary walls to ensure a safe and secure environment. We're committed to responsible development, featuring a separate multiple drainage system with a sewage treatment plant.

Unmatched Connectivity

Situated in Mohania connected with Ranjhi having easy access to the upcoming highway connecting Panagar and Kundam.

Peaceful Worship Space

We have designed our Temple to be within an orchard full of sacred trees. It also has a peaceful space for assembly, yoga, meditation, and praying. It's a sacred place for everyone to find inner peace.

Smart Colony of Smart City

Live with confidence in a well-equipped community. Gujral Hills provides essential infrastructure you can trust, like paved roads, efficient drainage systems, and reliable utilities for electricity and water.

Family-Friendly Living

We have everything to make your family happy. The kids will love their play area as there's plenty of space to relax and play together. Embrace an active lifestyle with our jogging track, open gym, and cricket pitch, all designed to encourage outdoor activities and physical well-being.

Modern Comfort Meets Convenience at Gujral Hills

Looking for a better life in Ranjhi without leaving your roots behind? Gujral Hills is the answer. Unlike the rest of Ranjhi, we're a meticulously planned township, offering a peaceful lifestyle with modern conveniences.
