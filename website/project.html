Jabalpur

Jabalpur, a city in Madhya Pradesh on the Narmada River, is the third-biggest city in the state and one of India's largest. It's the centre of the state in terms of administration and commerce.

The city serves as the judicial capital of Madhya Pradesh as well as the headquarters of the West Central Railway. The famous marble rocks of Bhedaghat tower over the Narmada. Jabalpur was chosen as one of India's first smart cities — locally nicknamed "Sanskardhani" meaning The Cultural Capital.
